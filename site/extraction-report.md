# About this collection

This gallery presents 195 nesoberi models across 26 characters and 29 costumes. Names are taken from the game's own data.

## What is included

- Matching standing character artwork and original circular costume badges.
- Four face-expression choices from the game's face textures. The small selector icons are illustrative; the selected expression uses the original texture.
- A costume display pedestal for each costume, with the original artwork.
- Recovered skill animation previews, matching skill voices and available sound effects for all 195 nesoberi. Use the sparkle button in the model view to play a skill.

## Model notes

The 3D models are static previews. Recovered skill animations play as a separate overlay over the darkened model view. Use Pause, Resume or Replay during playback, or close the animation to return to the model.

Sustained skills automatically play one complete loop before their ending. For held states, the preview uses the longest active repeating effect cycle.

The recovered portrait composition fits inside the model window without stretching. Some lighting, particles and material effects remain approximations for the web. These previews do not recreate the gameplay board. The pedestal shapes are reconstructed cylinders; their closed bottoms reuse the top artwork as a gallery addition.

## Source

Prepared from a local Android copy of Puchiguru Love Live. Character and costume names, artwork, badges, and textures come from the game's own data and assets.
